Services: we find the part, check it fits and bring it to you

Six steps of work we do for individual owners, garages and businesses. Every request is handled one by one.

Automotive parts sourcing

We sell car body parts from our shops and source other parts through our suppliers, including a supplier in China whose warehouse we have visited.

If you need an engine, brake, suspension, electrical or other part, send the details and we will try to find it.

Part identification assistance

Not sure what the part is called, or which one you need? We help you identify it from photos, the old part, or the vehicle details.

  • Send a photo of the old or damaged part from two angles.
  • Send the part number label if you can see one.
  • Send the chassis number from the vehicle plate or papers.

Compatibility investigation

Before we quote, we check that the part is made for your make, model, year and trim. Parts that look the same can differ between versions of the same car.

We tell you if we are not sure, and what we need to find out.

Supplier quotation coordination

We contact our suppliers for availability, condition and price, then give you one clear quotation instead of you chasing several.

Where there are choices, such as genuine, aftermarket or used, we show them side by side.

Garage and business sourcing support

Mechanics, garages, workshops and fleet operators can send a list of parts they order often and ask about business arrangements.

See garages and business customers

Procurement and delivery coordination

After you approve the quotation, we place the order with the supplier, follow it up, and arrange delivery or collection.

We deliver to all parts of Ghana. Cost and timing depend on the part and your location.

What we confirm for every request

No two requests are the same. For each one we confirm, in writing in the chat, before you pay:

  • Availability and how long it will take
  • Compatibility with your vehicle
  • The total price
  • The condition of the part: genuine, aftermarket or used
  • Any warranty or return terms
  • Delivery or collection arrangements

To get an accurate quotation quickly, have these ready:

  • Vehicle make, model and year
  • Chassis number, if you have it
  • The part you need, and its number if known
  • Photos of the old part and the vehicle
  • Where the part must be delivered
