How a request works

From your first message to the part in your hands, in five steps.

  1. Send the details

    Tell us the vehicle, the part and where it must go. Use the form or WhatsApp.

  2. We identify the part

    We check the part number and fitment against your vehicle. A chassis number helps.

  3. We get quotations

    Our suppliers confirm availability, condition and price.

  4. You approve the cost

    We confirm the total, including delivery, before anything is ordered.

  5. We deliver

    We arrange delivery or collection and keep you updated.

Questions we get asked

How do I request a part?

Fill in the request form and we open WhatsApp for you with your details filled in. You can also message or call us directly. Photos of the part and your vehicle help a lot.

Do you have the part in stock?

Some body parts are on display in our shops. Stock changes, so we confirm availability for your request. If we do not have it, we ask our suppliers and tell you how long it will take.

How do you decide the price?

We get a quotation from the supplier, add our costs and delivery, and tell you the total. The price, the condition of the part and any warranty are confirmed with you before you pay.

What if I do not know the part number?

That is common. Send photos of the old part and your vehicle, and the chassis number if you have it. We will work out the part and check that it fits.

Can you deliver outside Kumasi and Accra?

Yes. We arrange shipping and delivery to all parts of Ghana. Cost and timing depend on the part and the location, and we confirm them for each order.

Do you sell new, used or aftermarket parts?

It depends on the part and the supplier. Tell us what you prefer on the request form. We will say clearly what each quotation is, so you can compare.

Do you work with garages and fleets?

Yes. See garages and business customers to send a regular parts list and ask about arrangements.
