Privacy notice

How we handle the details you share with us. Last updated 22 October 2026.

Who we are

K. Agyemang Auto Parts is an automotive parts business in Ghana with shops in Suame, Kumasi and Abossey Okai, Accra.

What this website collects

Nothing. The request forms on this website build a message inside your own browser and open WhatsApp. The website does not store or send your form details to us or to anyone else. Your request reaches us only when you press Send in WhatsApp.

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics tools. Fonts, images and videos are hosted on the site itself.

Details you send us

When you message us on WhatsApp, call us, or contact us through Instagram, LinkedIn or WeChat, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, vehicle details, the part you need and your delivery location.

  • We use them to identify the part, get quotations, agree a price with you and arrange delivery.
  • We share only what is needed with suppliers and delivery providers.
  • We do not sell your details.

Other services

Links to WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn and WeChat take you to services run by other companies. They have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can ask us what details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them. Contact us on WhatsApp or by phone using the details on the contact page.

Changes

If we change how this website works, for example by adding an online form service, we will update this notice before the change goes live.
