A family business, building a more organised way to source parts

K. Agyemang Auto Parts sells car body parts in Kumasi and Accra and finds the rest for you.

Our story

K. Agyemang Auto Parts is a Ghanaian family business, established in 1995. It was started by David’s father and today David Kwame Yeboah Agyemang manages it. The K in our name stands for Kwame.

We sell car body parts from our shops in Suame, Kumasi and Abossey Okai, Accra. When a customer needs something we do not carry, we source it through our suppliers and deliver it anywhere in Ghana.

Customers have always asked us the same thing: can you get this part, and what will it cost? We are making that answer faster and clearer, with a proper request process, honest quotations and suppliers we have met in person.

How we work

We say what we know

Availability, compatibility, price, condition and warranty are confirmed for each request. If we are unsure, we tell you.

We go to the source

We visit our suppliers, see the stock and meet the people we buy from.

We stay reachable

You can reach us by WhatsApp, phone or in person at our shops in Suame and Abossey Okai.
