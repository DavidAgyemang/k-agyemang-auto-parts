Car body parts and parts sourcing in Ghana since 1995

K. Agyemang Auto Parts sells car body parts in Kumasi and Accra and finds the rest for you.

Our story

K. Agyemang Auto Parts was founded in 1995 by Mr. Francis Agyemang. We sell car body parts from our shops in Suame, Kumasi and Abossey Okai, Accra, and source other parts on request through our supplier network. We deliver anywhere in Ghana.

Customers ask us the same two things: can you get this part, and what will it cost? We answer with a clear request process, honest quotations and suppliers we have visited in person.

Founder: Mr. Francis Agyemang

Our mission

To supply quality car body parts and reliable parts sourcing at fair prices, and to confirm every detail with the customer before they pay.

Our vision

To be the most trusted name in car body parts and parts sourcing in Ghana.
